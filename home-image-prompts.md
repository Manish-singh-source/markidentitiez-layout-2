# Home Page Image Prompts

Use these prompts for replacing or upgrading the key homepage visuals.

## 1. Hero Video Direction

Create a premium branding agency hero video sequence showing close-up logo sketching, typography layout boards, packaging mockups, color palette systems, editorial brand presentations, and polished digital brand applications. Keep the mood cinematic, strategic, refined, and modern. Use a dark charcoal base with warm off-white and muted green accents. Avoid generic office scenes, handshake visuals, or random stock marketing footage.

## 2. Positioning Section Image

Create a luxury branding strategy presentation board featuring logo construction sketches, typography hierarchy sheets, color swatches, visual moodboards, stationery mockups, and brand guideline fragments. The composition should feel editorial, elegant, and highly intentional, with premium studio lighting and a modern black, ivory, and muted green palette.

## 3. Featured Case Study Image

Create a high-end branding case study board showing a before-and-after brand transformation, premium packaging mockups, logo applications, stationery, social media previews, and presentation slides arranged in a sharp editorial grid. The image should look like a real agency portfolio board, clean, realistic, and market-ready.

## 4. Packaging Showcase Image

Create a premium packaging design showcase with box, pouch, bottle, and label mockups arranged in a sophisticated studio composition. Include subtle strategic annotations, elegant typography, realistic material textures, and a shelf-ready premium aesthetic. Avoid cartoonish colors or overly playful styling.

## 5. Brand Identity System Image

Create a complete brand identity system presentation showing logo variations, monogram, typography hierarchy, color palette, pattern system, stationery mockups, digital UI previews, and guideline snippets. Style it like a luxury design agency presentation board with clean lighting, editorial layout, and modern realism.
